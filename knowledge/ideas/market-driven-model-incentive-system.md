# Market-Driven Model Incentive and Royalty System

## Idea

An economic protocol and credit/payment mechanism where creators of quantitative models, equations, and data projections earn recurring compensation (credits, micro-royalties, or API fees) based on the usage and invocation of their nodes within downstream computational graphs.

The more foundational and reliable a model is—and the more frequently it is embedded by third parties or executed by autonomous agents—the greater the rewards accrued by its creator.

## Why it may matter

- **Overcoming the proprietary barrier**: Many of the world's most valuable models are currently proprietary or locked inside academic silos. Providing a monetization mechanism allows authors to commercialize their intellectual property without exposing their raw source code (e.g. running behind zero-knowledge or confidential execution envelopes).
- **Network flywheel**: Compensating model authors directly incentivizes the organic expansion and maintenance of the global model repository, solving the cold-start data acquisition problem.

## What would make it worth pursuing?

- Achieving an operational multi-engine runtime where models can be executed securely and metered accurately.
- Demonstrating willing commercial payers (e.g. hedge funds, enterprise planning teams, or autonomous agent providers) paying for high-assurance model evaluations.
