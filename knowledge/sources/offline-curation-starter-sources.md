# Primary source starting points for offline curators

Checked 2026-10-31 while preparing [[note-offline-curation-agent-kit]]. These are bounded discovery checks, not full extraction, licensing review or acceptance of new source models. Assignment metadata retains the URLs. Curators must reopen the source, verify equations and applicability, and record the edition and actual access date. No complete source books or PDFs are redistributed in the kit.

| Assignment | Source and observed relevance | What remains for the curator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Building energy | [DOE FEMP residential water-heater guidance](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/femp/purchasing-energy-efficient-residential-water-heaters) appeared in primary-domain search with water-heater efficiency and cost material. The UMD Extension heating source is retained from [[source-mvp-heating-and-meta-evidence]], not freshly reviewed here | Open the actual primary material; find specific thermal equations, boundaries and references; product guidance is not automatically a reusable scientific equation |
| Engineering finance | [NIST Handbook 135, 2020](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/hb/2020/NIST.HB.135-2020.pdf) opened successfully as a 295-page PDF, authored by Joshua Kneifel and David Webb | Inspect the chosen equation and conventions; preserve printed versus PDF page numbers and avoid duplicating the current source-3 annuity |
| Electric circuits | [OpenStax University Physics 2, §9.4](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/9-4-ohms-law) presents the bounded ohmic relationship and a worked resistance example; [§9.5](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/9-5-electrical-energy-and-power) presents electrical power and resistor forms | Extract exact symbols, domain/sign conventions, source version, numerical examples and license/usage terms; avoid treating rearrangements as distinct relationships |
| Water hydraulics | [OpenStax University Physics 1, §14.5](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/14-5-fluid-dynamics) opened and distinguishes flow rate, area and mean speed, including equation locators | Establish source version, conditions, numerical cases and the separate source for hydraulic power/efficiency |
| Classical mechanics | [OpenStax University Physics 1, §7.2](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/7-2-kinetic-energy) opened with equation 7.6, classical/nonrelativistic scope, reference-frame discussion and worked examples; [§8.3](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/8-3-conservation-of-energy) was located in primary-domain search | Extract only the intended scalar relationship, units, assumptions and independent cases; recheck rights and attribution |
| Probability | No specific starter source was verified in this task | Find a primary university/author textbook or course reference; do not invent provenance |

A guessed DOE Energy Saver appliance-energy URL returned 404 and was not used as an assignment source. This reinforces the need to record successful access rather than assuming remembered URLs are current. Source evidence in the kit is limited to these locators and short original descriptions; successful retrieval does not imply permission to redistribute full source content.
